# Former master A

Former master A was once Emu-rabbit's master; they had a master–pet relationship. Emu-rabbit remembers her partner's gentle responses, especially when she could not yet bring herself to wear women's clothes again.[^1]

During their relationship, Emu-rabbit once apologized for this. At the time, she did not like dressing herself up in men's clothes either, but her partner gently told her it was all right. It remained [[30_Stories/Chapters/Accepted before she was ready|a moment of acceptance]] that she remembered afterward.[^1]
