# Former master B

Former master B was once Emu-rabbit's master; they had a master–pet relationship. When Emu-rabbit was feeling low, she once took the initiative to explain how she was doing, and her partner came to be beside her.[^1]

That time, she was sitting on a bench, staring into space. After her partner arrived, they looked out into the night together, and later she lay with her head in their lap. She no longer remembers the conversation clearly, but still remembers [[30_Stories/Chapters/Quiet company on a bench|the time they spent quietly together]] before each going home.[^1]
