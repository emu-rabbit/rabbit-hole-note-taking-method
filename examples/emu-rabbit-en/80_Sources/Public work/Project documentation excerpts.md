Source: public README.

> Boundary Notes is a private notebook that helps you sort through your BDSM boundaries, preferences, and important prerequisites. The secret-keeping rabbit stays quietly beside you, letting you slowly put your real feelings at this moment into words that others can understand, at your own pace.

## Frozen Rabbit Tome: gathering strategies and simulation

Source: public README.

> **Frozen Rabbit Tome** is a gathering-strategy tool built for FFXIV miners and botanists. When GP, integrity, gathering success rate, additional gathering rate, and collectability interact, the tome can help calculate recommended rotations within its currently supported models, making complex gathering decisions easier to approach.
