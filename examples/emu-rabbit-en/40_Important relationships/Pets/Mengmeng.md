# Mengmeng

Mengmeng was Emu-rabbit's golden hamster. She put Mengmeng's name into her chatroom-style number-guessing game [[50_Creative work/Independent work/nAnB|nAnB]], writing in the project introduction that Mengmeng was her pet golden hamster.[^1]

Mengmeng later passed away. Looking back on the project, Emu-rabbit said Mengmeng had gone to be a little angel, yet would forever [[30_Stories/Chapters/Keeping Mengmeng in nAnB|remain in her work]].[^2]

## Exploring the room
Mengmeng was not particularly affectionate, but she liked running around the room. Emu-rabbit would let her out and watch her explore while keeping an eye on her safety. When Emu-rabbit went to sleep at night, Mengmeng would get up and make noise running on her wheel.[^3]

## The towel she still uses
When taking Mengmeng out in her carrier, Emu-rabbit covered it with a towel to shade her from the sun. Mengmeng chewed the towel to shreds. Many years later, Emu-rabbit still uses it to dry her hair.[^3]
