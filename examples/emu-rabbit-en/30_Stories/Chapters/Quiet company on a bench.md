# Quiet company on a bench

Once, when Emu-rabbit was not doing well, she sat alone on a bench, staring into space. She took the initiative to tell her then-master, [[40_Important people/People/Former master B|Former master B]], how she was feeling, and her partner came to keep her company.[^1]

They looked out into the night. She no longer remembers what they talked about. Later, she lay with her head in her partner's lap, and they stayed together like that for a while before each going home.[^1]
