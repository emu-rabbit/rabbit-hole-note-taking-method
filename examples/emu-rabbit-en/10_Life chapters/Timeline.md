# Timeline

## 2011–2014: High school
She researched the four-color problem and gradually shifted her focus toward computing. Exceptionally capable classmates made her feel pressure, but they still shared close bonds. She also wrote modern poetry and a recitation text for her class.

## 2014–2018: University
She liked rules and simulation, wanted to write a computer inside a computer, and made an infinite hotel assignment using linked lists and big numbers. Both attempts had unfinished parts.
[[10_Life chapters/02_2014-2018_Making curiosity work|Making curiosity work]]

## 2019–2020: Building for work and games
Her one-click reporting extension for operations was used by the entire team. She also developed FoodPower and made LinkArray in 2020. A stable life and small achievements helped her gradually regain confidence.
[[10_Life chapters/03_2019-2020_Finding reasons to make things|Finding reasons to make things]]

## 2021–2024: Clothing and learning
She attended a work party in clothes she liked, telling people it was not cosplay; she simply liked wearing women's clothes. In her learning, she moved beyond the frontend into Flutter, Firebase, and basic color design.
[[10_Life chapters/04_2021-2024_Bringing what she loves into daily life|Bringing what she loves into daily life]]

## 2025–2026: Creative work and self-expression
She made FF14 tools, Boundary Notes, a personal website, and a macaron portfolio. AI accelerated her creative work, and she also became more concerned with preserving her own words and ideas.
