# Complete a saved piece of work

Use this workflow after an interview, a read import batch or an authorized maintenance pass. It is the agent's responsibility within the agreed task, rather than a sequence the owner needs to request. A setup-only, archive-only, check-only or conversation-only request keeps its narrower scope.

## Preserve and understand

Preserve new evidence through the vault's source workflow before composing from it. An imported record already has a stable source locator; a manual interview needs its speaker, narration date, event uncertainty and passage anchors. Keep original wording and edited interpretation distinguishable.

Read complete relevant passages and existing owning pages. Identify who did what for whom, whether it happened or remained an intention, and how precisely it can be dated. Keep disagreements attributed and unresolved details open. Ask a focused question only when it would materially change the work and the evidence cannot resolve it.

## Integrate the account

Reuse existing relationship, story and work identities. Keep a short account together when that reads well; add a page when the material can support an independently useful article.

- A story introduces people and circumstances, then lets the scene unfold.
- A relationship note introduces a person, pet, emotionally significant doll or relationship circle and follows the documented connection over time.
- A life period or topic brings particular experiences together without inventing causal connections.
- A work page explains what was made, why, its development and each contributor's role.
- A favorite preserves what the person enjoys and the experience or feeling that gives it meaning.

Write in the owner's chosen voice, third person by default. Let ordinary life, concrete details and unfinished attempts carry the account. A newcomer should understand each article without first reading the whole vault. Put source footnotes beside supported claims, linking to exact existing passages; place detailed reconciliation where it helps rather than making it the story's opening.

## Make it findable

Update the affected entrance, source index, lookup entries and chronology as the material warrants. Keep titles and short summaries consistent with the updated account, including its event state and date certainty. Keep roles distinct: README welcomes, a profile introduces, an index supports lookup, a reading map offers short journeys. These can share a page while the vault is small.

Add natural first-mention links to people, stories and works. Choose a meaningful next stop and return path, keeping article endings natural and optional source detours clear. Use actual targets; record undeveloped ideas as questions until they have content.

Record source availability separately from the passages actually read and integrated. Leave a precise next range or a useful optional interview question. For substantial changes of understanding, record the earlier view, evidence, revision and remaining uncertainty in maintenance.

## Read and verify

Run the read-only [checker](cli.md). Compare changed claims with their full source passages, then reread every changed article as a newcomer. Check background, transitions, speaker attribution, event state and date precision. Revise a record dump, an unsupported character judgment or a forced life lesson into a readable supported account.

Walk from README or the appropriate index to the result, its cited passages and back. Try a real reader question about the new material. Repair confusing prose or navigation even when links technically pass; repeat the affected read and check after repair.

Finish with a brief useful result, its entrance and any consequential uncertainty. Keep the technical record in the vault; the owner does not need a narrated checklist or another skill invocation to finish the work.
